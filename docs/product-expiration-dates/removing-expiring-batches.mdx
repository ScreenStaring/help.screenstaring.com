# Automatically Removing Expiring Inventory Batches

Removing inventory batches based on their expiration date can be done by selecting "Remove expiring inventory from Shopify" from the <Link path="/settings#inventory">Inventory tab on the settings page</Link> or as part of [Ruleset](/docs/product-expiration-dates/discounts#rulesets) with a Remove Inventor Batch Action.
