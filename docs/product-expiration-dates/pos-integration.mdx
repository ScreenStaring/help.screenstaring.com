# Shopify Point of Sale (POS) Integration

***Shopify POS integration is only available under the <Link path="/charges/recurring/new">Platinum Plan</Link>***

Our Shopify POS integration allows inventory batches to be assigned to orders from within the Shopify POS. To install, add the Product Expiration Dates app as a tile on the POS home screen:

1.  From the POS home screen touch "Add tile" and select "App":
    
    <img src="/product-expiration-dates/shopify/images/pos-tile-step-1.jpg" height="500" alt="Add app as a tile" />
    
2.  From the app list select "SS: Product Expiration Dates":
    
    <img src="/product-expiration-dates/shopify/images/pos-tile-step-2.jpg" height="500" alt="Find the Product Expiration Dates app" />
    
3.  Next to "Assign expiration data to products" click "Add":
    
    <img src="/product-expiration-dates/shopify/images/pos-tile-step-3.jpg" height="500" alt="Add the Product Expiration Dates app" />
    
4.  This will show the "SS: Product Expiration Dates" tile on the home page:
    
    <img src="/product-expiration-dates/shopify/images/pos-tile-step-4.jpg" height="500" alt="Save the tile" />
    
5.  Touch "Save"

Now when items are in the cart you can touch the "SS: Product Expiration Dates" tile and assign inventory batches. Batches shown will be those from the POS' location.
