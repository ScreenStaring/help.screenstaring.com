# Viewing Orders' Dates & Batch Numbers in Shopify

You can view the inventory batches assigned to an order from the order's page in the Shopify Admin by adding the Expiration Dates block to the page.

1.  Open your order in the [the Shopify admin](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/orders/manage-orders#view-an-order)
2.  On the order's page scroll to the bottom and select "Add block"

    <img src="/product-expiration-dates/shopify/images/admin-orders-add-block.jpg" width="700" alt="Select Expiration Dates block in Shopify Admin" />

3.  Select "Expiration Dates" from the popup menu
4.  This will show the expiration dates assigned to the order's line items, if any. Note that only orders **after** September 11th, 2026 will have their dates shown:

    <img src="/product-expiration-dates/shopify/images/admin-orders-batches-block.jpg" width="700" alt="Expiration Dates shown in the Shopify Admin" />

5.  To make the block always appear on the order's page click the pin icon:

    <img src="/product-expiration-dates/shopify/images/admin-orders-pin-block.jpg" width="700" alt="Pin the Expiration Dates block to the Shopify Admin" />

You can also open the order's page in the app:

1.  Open your order in the [the Shopify admin](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/orders/manage-orders#view-an-order)
2.  On the order's page select "More actions"
3.  Select "Expiration Dates"

Batch numbers can also be seen on the order's page in the Shopify Admin under the Additional Details section.
