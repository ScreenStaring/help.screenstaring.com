# Folate, Folic Acid

Folic acid is displayed directly below folate when both are present:

:::info
This option is available for the "US Nutrition Facts – Vertical" and "US Supplement Facts – Vertical" label formats. It has no effect unless the label lists both folate and folic acid.
:::

1. Find the desired product and click the "Edit" button
1. Go to the Label Display Settings section
1. Select "Nutrition Panel"
1. Check "Group folate and folic acid"

   <div>

1. Click <kbd>Update</kbd>

This follows the FDA's <a href="https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-facts-label/folate-and-folic-acid-nutrition-and-supplement-facts-labels" target="_blank">labeling guidance for folate and folic acid</a>.
