# Amino Acids

Amino acids can be moved out of the nutrient list and into their own section at the bottom of the panel:

:::info
This option is only available for Supplement Facts label formats. You can set a default on <Link path="/settings/edit#label-display-settings">your account's settings page</Link>
:::

1. Find the desired product and click the "Edit" button
1. Go to the Label Display Settings section
1. Select "Nutrition Panel"
1. Check "Create separate section for amino acids"
   {/* Image temporarily disabled: /itsgot/shopify/images/label-separate-amino-acids.jpg not available
   <div>
     <img src="/itsgot/shopify/images/label-separate-amino-acids.jpg" width="700" alt="Creating a Separate Amino Acids Section" />
   </div>
   */}
1. Click <kbd>Update</kbd>

The section is headed "Amino Acids" and is only shown if the product has amino acids.
