# Frequently Asked Questions


Don't see your question here? Please contact us. We'll be happy to assist.

## Labels Are Not Available in My Language or Country's Format

Please [contact our support team](https://screenstaring.com/#contact) and we can add any necessary translations and/or format(s).

## Label Is Partially Visible or Does Not Completely Load

This is normally caused when the label is inserted within a tabs, accordion, or similar widget. In most cases you can fix this by setting the
*Default insertion delay* field on the <Link path="/settings/edit">settings page</Link>.

The proper delay varies by site but usually setting it to 3.5 seconds will fix things.
